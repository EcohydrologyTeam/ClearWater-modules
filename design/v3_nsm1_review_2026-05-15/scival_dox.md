## 1. Verdict

The v3 DOX science is largely correct and is built on canonical, defensible formulations. The DO-saturation equation is the APHA/Benson-Krause 1984 four-coefficient natural-log polynomial with the standard pressure, water-vapor, and theta correction; every coefficient matches the published form to the digit. The reaeration menu reproduces the QUAL2K hydraulic and wind option set with coefficients that are internally consistent with the QUAL2K manual (Chapra, Pelletier, and Tao 2008). The reaeration temperature factor theta = 1.024 matches CE-QUAL-W2 and Chapra (1997) exactly. The O2:N nitrification ratio (4.5714) and O2:C oxidation ratio (2.6667) match stoichiometry exactly. The SOD form is a defensible zero-order areal flux with Arrhenius and an optional DO-Monod attenuation, and the v3 corrected defaults (SOD_20 = 1.0, SOD_theta = 1.060) are within Bowie et al. (1985) and Chapra (1997) ranges and repair a catastrophic v1 sentinel defect (999).

Two issues are material and are not v3-introduced; they are shared v1-and-v3 properties that this review is specifically chartered to surface:

1. V1&V3-BOTH: salinity correction on DO saturation is omitted. Documented as a freshwater assumption (audit C6). CE-QUAL-W2 `SATO` includes it (`w2modules.F90:370`). Correct for freshwater NSM1; a real defect for any brackish or estuarine coupling, and silent (no guard, no warning).
2. V1&V3-BOTH: the QUAL2K hydraulic reaeration menu in v1/v3 uses a different empirical formula family than CE-QUAL-W2's RIVER menu. This is not an error in either model; it is two valid empirical menus. But the v3 documentation attributes options to the wrong primary authors in two places (option 3 and the named "Cover/Covar 1976"), which is a documentation-to-code fidelity defect that can mislead a modeler selecting a formula by author name.
3. DOX-F1 residual (the parity-round Major): with the shipped defaults the zero-reaeration short-circuit does not fire and reaeration is computed normally. The residual silent-zero risk under explicit `hydraulic_reaeration_option = 1` with `kah_20_user = 0.0` is confirmed and is genuinely divergent from CE-QUAL-W2, which always evaluates a formula (W2 has no user-zero path that silently disables reaeration; its NEQN = 0 is a real Covar-style formula and its minimum-value floor `MINKL` further prevents zero). This is reclassified below from MAJOR to a confirmed V3-DIVERGENT science finding with a concrete safer behavior drawn from W2.

No Critical science defect was found. The DO chemistry is sound. The findings are one confirmed shared scientific limitation (salinity), one confirmed divergence with a validated-model precedent (silent-zero reaeration vs W2's always-compute-plus-floor), and documentation-attribution defects that matter for correct formula selection.

Findings by severity: Critical 0, Major 2, Minor 3, Observation 3.

## 4. DO-saturation coefficient audit

v3 `dox_sat_apha` (`dox.py:185-201`) uncorrected term:

```
ln(DOsat) = -139.34410 + 1.575701e5/Tk - 6.642308e7/Tk^2 + 1.243800e10/Tk^3 - 8.621949e11/Tk^4
```

These five coefficients are the APHA Standard Methods / Benson & Krause (1984) saturation polynomial as reproduced in Chapra (1997) Eq. 33-x and the QUAL2E manual (Brown & Barnwell 1987). They match the published values to all printed digits. The pressure correction structure `Patm*(1 - Pwv/Patm)*(1 - theta*Patm)/((1 - Pwv)*(1 - theta))` is the APHA pressure correction with `Pwv` the partial pressure of water vapor and `theta` (here named `alpha`) the empirical scaled-density coefficient; both the `pwv` polynomial (`11.8571 - 3840.70/Tk - 216961/Tk^2`) and the `alpha` polynomial (`0.000975 - 1.426e-5 Tc + 6.436e-8 Tc^2`) are the APHA published forms (Zison et al. 1978; APHA). The mb-to-atm factor `0.000986923` is correct (1 mb = 0.000986923 atm).

CE-QUAL-W2's `SATO` (`w2modules.F90:368`) uses a completely different equation: `EXP(7.7117 - 1.31403*LN(T+45.93))*P`. This is the Mortimer (1981) / American Public Health older two-coefficient log fit, not the Benson-Krause 4-coefficient polynomial. The two curves agree to within roughly 0.1 mg/L over 0-30 deg C, so this is a STRUCTURAL-OK divergence (two accepted published curves), not an error. v3's choice (Benson-Krause) is the more modern and the one QUAL2K and WASP also use, so v3 is well-aligned with the water-quality-model mainstream.

Salinity: v3 omits the salinity correction. CE-QUAL-W2 includes it (`w2modules.F90:370`) as `EXP(LOG(SATO) - SAL*(1.7674e-2 - 1.0754e1/Tk + 2.1407e3/Tk^2))`. The three salinity coefficients in W2 (1.7674e-2, 1.0754e1, 2.1407e3) are exactly the APHA/Benson-Krause salinity coefficients. v3 simply does not apply this factor. For S = 0 (fresh water) the factor is exactly 1.0, so v3 is numerically correct for the freshwater NSM1 use case. The defect is that the omission is silent: there is no salinity input, no guard, and no docstring note (see F1). At S = 35 ppt and 20 deg C the omission overstates DOsat by roughly 18 percent, which would systematically bias the reaeration driving force `(DOsat - DOX)` for any brackish or estuarine application.

Pressure/altitude: present and correct (the `pressure_mb` input flows through `Patm`). A user at altitude must supply the reduced `pressure_mb`; there is no internal altitude-to-pressure conversion, which matches v1 and is acceptable.

VERDICT: V3-DIVERGENT; recommended fix is to (a) emit a one-time warning when the user-zero hydraulic path is taken, and (b) optionally adopt a W2-style `MINKL` minimum-reaeration floor parameter (default 0, opt-in) so production runs cannot silently lose the atmospheric source.
